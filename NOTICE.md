## Data: FLORES+

`data/flores_plus/` contains sentences from **FLORES+** (Open Language Data Initiative), which is licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
FLORES+ sentences are drawn from Wikinews, Wikijunior and Wikivoyage.

- Source: https://github.com/openlanguagedata/flores
- Files under `data/flores_plus/raw/` are subsets of FLORES+ (Korean/English/German/French/Italian/Japanese/Spanish, as selected by the author).
- Files under `data/flores_plus/processed/` and `data/flores_plus/resources/` (translation memory, terminology, Do-Not-Edit lists) were derived from those subsets by the author's scripts in `scripts/`.
  Derived data stays under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
